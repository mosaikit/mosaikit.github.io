Official plugins

The plugins in mosaikit-plugins. Each is a .mskp package the Administrator installs from the Plugins page; the platform installs only packages signed with a trusted key. Versions are those of the repository today.

Applications

Documents 0.5.3

Shareable files and folders, with preview, zip and public links.

Notes 0.7.1

Personal notes, shareable with colleagues and groups.

Tasks 0.4.4

Tasks with due date and priority, assigned by sharing them.

Contacts 0.4.3

Shareable address book, with normalized and validated emails.

Maps 0.6.1

Catalog of GeoLibre maps: the configuration is saved in the .geolibre.json project.

Sites 0.8.2

Publishes static sites from zip archives or shows a site by its web address, shareable and with public links.

Note review 0.2.1

Marks notes as reviewed, with who did it and the history of the reviews.

Layouts

How the interface is arranged: pick one per tenant.

Project 1.0.1

Global navigation at the left, the sidebar of the area next to it.

Suite 1.0.1

Apps from a launcher grid, the pages of the area in a bar under the band.

Tabs 1.0.1

Apps and pages as tabs under the band, a menu for the pages of an app.

Workspace 1.0.1

Top bar with a wide search, a drawer that opens and closes.

Languages

Spanish 0.1.7

A sample language plugin: the interface in Spanish, with a starter set of texts (sign-in page, navigation, Notes).
