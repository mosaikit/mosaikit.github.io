Official plugins

The plugins in mosaikit-plugins. Each is a .mskp package the Administrator installs from the Plugins page; the platform installs only packages signed with a trusted key. Versions are those of the repository today: 31 plugins. Select Details on a card for the full description, typical uses and screenshots.

Applications

Documents 0.5.3

Files and folders the whole team can reach, with the same sharing as everything else in Mosaikit.

Notes 0.9.1

Personal notes written in Markdown (bold, lists, check lists with a progress bar) that look like coloured post-its.

real-time

Tasks 0.6.0

A to-do manager with a quick-add line, a list grouped by due date, a board and a week view you can drag tasks across.

Contacts 0.5.0

A shared address book with cards or an A to Z list, tags, birthdays, normalised and validated emails and phone numbers.

Chat 0.1.0

Conversations in channels that are shared like any other item: whoever can read a channel follows it, whoever can write talks.

real-time

Meet 0.1.0

Video meeting rooms that open inside the page, with camera, microphone and screen sharing, shared like any item so you decide who can enter.

real-timecamera and microphone

Announcements 0.1.1

A notice board for the people you share it with: publish a notice with a title, a text in Markdown, a level (information, important or urgent) and the period it is valid.

Bookmarks 0.1.0

A shared collection of web links: each bookmark has a title, an address, a note and tags.

Snippets 0.1.0

A shared collection of reusable texts and pieces of code: give each one a title, a kind (plain text, shell, SQL, JSON, JavaScript, Python, Rust or Markdown) and tags, then find it by search, kind or tag and copy it to the clipboard in one click.

Polls 0.2.0

A quick question to a group: write the question and two to twelve options, choose single or multiple choice, set an optional closing date and decide whether the voters are named.

real-time

Surveys 0.1.1

A series of questions answered by the people a survey is shared with: one answer, several answers, free text or a scale from 1 to 5.

Approvals 0.1.1

A request that one or more people must approve or reject, with the history of who decided what and when.

real-time

Dashboard 0.1.0

One page you arrange from widgets that show what matters to you across the apps: your open and overdue tasks, notices waiting for your confirmation, polls to vote, requests to decide, your recent notes.

Forms 0.1.0

A form builder: add fields (text, long text, one or several choices, number, date, email), mark the required ones and share the form.

Projects 0.1.1

Give a set of tasks a plan.

Calendar 0.1.1

The agenda of the team.

Whiteboard 0.1.1

A shared free-form board for the people you share it with: sticky notes, rectangles, ellipses, text, arrows and freehand drawing on an endless canvas.

real-time

Maps 0.6.1

A catalogue of GeoLibre maps: each map is a project that you open, edit and share.

Sites 0.8.0

Publishes a static site from a zip archive, or shows an existing site by its web address, inside the page.

Extensions

Small additions to an application.

Note review 0.2.1

An extension for Notes: mark a note as reviewed, with who did it and when, and see the history of the reviews of each note.

Layouts

How the interface is arranged: pick one per tenant.

Project 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: the global navigation sits at the left and the sidebar of the area next to it.

Suite 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: apps open from a launcher grid and the pages of the area sit in a bar under the band, like an office suite.

Tabs 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: apps and pages are tabs under the band and a menu holds the pages of an app, like a browser.

Workspace 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: a top bar with a wide search and a drawer that opens and closes, to give the content the whole screen.
