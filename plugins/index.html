Official plugins

The plugins in mosaikit-plugins. Each is a .mskp package the Administrator installs from the Plugins page; the platform installs only packages signed with a trusted key. Versions are those of the repository today: 14 plugins, each with its card and screenshots.

Applications

Documents 0.5.3

Shareable files and folders, with preview, zip and public links.

Store files and folders, preview them in the browser, download a folder as a zip archive and share them.

Notes 0.9.0

Personal notes in Markdown with check lists, pins and an archive, shareable with colleagues and groups.

Write notes in Markdown (bold, lists, check lists with progress) as coloured post-its with a title, a text and tags of your own, resize them, keep them private or share them with colleagues and groups.

real-time

Tasks 0.5.0

Tasks with due date, priority, labels, projects, checklists and repetition, assigned by sharing them.

Keep a list of things to do, each with a due date and a priority.

Contacts 0.5.0

Shareable address book with cards, tags, birthdays, vCard and CSV, and normalized, validated emails.

A shared address book. Email addresses are normalized and validated when you save a contact, so the book stays clean.

Chat 0.1.0

Channels and live conversations with presence, replies, reactions and Markdown.

Talk with your colleagues in channels. A message arrives at once for everyone who has the channel open, with who is online and who is typing.

real-time

Meet 0.1.0

Video meeting rooms: join with camera, microphone and screen share, see who is in the room.

Open a room and meet your colleagues face to face: camera, microphone, screen sharing and a chat inside the call.

real-timecamera and microphone

Maps 0.6.1

Catalog of GeoLibre maps: configuration saved in the .geolibre.json project.

A catalog of maps built with GeoLibre. Each map keeps its configuration (layers, styles, view) in a .geolibre.json project that you can open, edit and share.

Sites 0.8.2

Publishes static sites from zip archives or shows a site by its web address, shareable and with public links.

Publish a static site from a zip archive, or show an existing site by its web address inside the platform.

Extensions

Small additions to an application.

Note review 0.2.1

Marks notes as reviewed, with who did it and the history of the reviews.

Adds a Review tab to the notes of the Notes app. A reviewer marks a note as reviewed, with an optional comment; the tab shows who did it, when, and the history of the reviews.

Layouts

How the interface is arranged: pick one per tenant.

Project 1.0.1

Global navigation at the left, the sidebar of the area next to it.

Global navigation in a narrow rail at the left, with the pages of the current area in a sidebar next to it. Suited to people who work mostly in one area at a time.

Suite 1.0.1

Apps from a launcher grid, the pages of the area in a bar under the band.

Apps from a launcher grid, like an office suite; the pages of the area sit in a bar under the band. Suited to many apps used for short moments.

Tabs 1.0.1

Apps and pages as tabs under the band, a menu for the pages of an app.

Apps and pages as tabs under the band; a menu lists the pages of an app. Suited to few apps used side by side.

Workspace 1.0.1

Top bar with a wide search, a drawer that opens and closes.

A top bar with a wide search field and a drawer that opens and closes. Suited to people who search more than they browse.

Languages

Spanish 0.1.7

Spanish interface: a sample language plugin with a starter set of texts (sign-in page, navigation, Notes).

Spanish interface for the sign-in page, the navigation and the Notes app. It is a sample language plugin with a starter set of texts: whatever is not translated stays in English.
