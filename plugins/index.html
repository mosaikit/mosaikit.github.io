Official plugins

The plugins in mosaikit-plugins. Each is a .mskp package the Administrator installs from the Plugins page; the platform installs only packages signed with a trusted key. Versions are those of the repository today: 20 plugins. Select a picture to see its screenshots.

Applications

Documents 0.5.3

Files and folders the whole team can reach, with the same sharing as everything else in Mosaikit. Upload single files or whole folders, preview images, text and PDF in the browser, download a folder as a zip, unpack a zip into a tree of folders and give a file or folder a public link.

Typical uses

  • A shared archive of contracts, offers and minutes where each folder is shared with its own group
  • Sending a large deliverable to a customer with a public link that you can switch off
  • Keeping the receipts, scans and photos of a project next to the tasks that use them

Notes 0.9.0

Personal notes written in Markdown (bold, lists, check lists with a progress bar) that look like coloured post-its. Pin the ones that matter, archive the rest, share a note with colleagues and groups, export it as Markdown, and edit the same note live with other people.

Typical uses

  • Meeting minutes written together while the meeting is going on
  • A quick shopping or preparation list with boxes to tick
  • Ideas and drafts kept private until you decide to share them

real-time

Tasks 0.5.0

A to-do manager with a quick-add line, a list grouped by due date, a board and a week view you can drag tasks across. Give each task a priority, labels, a project, a check list and a repetition; assign it by sharing it and the person finds it in their own list.

Typical uses

  • Planning the week of a small team and seeing who has what
  • Recurring duties such as monthly reports or weekly checks that renew themselves
  • Breaking a job into a check list and following its progress

Contacts 0.5.0

A shared address book with cards or an A to Z list, tags, birthdays, normalised and validated emails and phone numbers. Import and export vCard and CSV, and find duplicates to merge.

Typical uses

  • A company address book every colleague sees and nobody keeps in a private spreadsheet
  • Moving contacts in from a phone or a mailing list and cleaning the doubles
  • Remembering the birthdays of customers and suppliers

Chat 0.1.0

Conversations in channels that are shared like any other item: whoever can read a channel follows it, whoever can write talks. Messages arrive at once with who is online and who is typing, Markdown, replies, reactions, editing and deleting, and a button that starts a video call in the channel.

Typical uses

  • A channel per project or customer, open to exactly the people involved
  • A quick question to the team without opening a mail thread
  • Jumping from a discussion into a call with one click

real-time

Meet 0.1.0

Video meeting rooms that open inside the page, with camera, microphone and screen sharing, shared like any item so you decide who can enter. The room shows who has it open; the call runs on the meeting service and no video goes through Mosaikit.

Typical uses

  • A standing daily meeting in a room everybody knows
  • A customer call from a room shared with the customer team only
  • A workshop with screen sharing for a distributed team

Maps 0.6.1

A catalogue of GeoLibre maps: each map is a project that you open, edit and share. The configuration is kept in the project file, so a map travels with its data and layers.

Typical uses

  • Keeping the maps of a territory or a site in one place with the right people allowed to edit
  • Sharing a read-only map with a colleague who only needs to look
  • Versioning the maps of a survey campaign

Sites 0.8.2

Publishes a static site from a zip archive, or shows an existing site by its web address, inside the page. Sites are shared like any item, can have a public link, a thumbnail and a card view, and a language change follows the host.

Typical uses

  • Publishing a documentation site or a landing page prepared elsewhere
  • Embedding an external dashboard or intranet page next to your tools
  • Handing a customer a private preview of a site with a link

Extensions

Small additions to an application.

Note review 0.2.1

An extension for Notes: mark a note as reviewed, with who did it and when, and see the history of the reviews of each note.

Typical uses

  • Procedures that must be read and approved by a second person
  • A trace of who checked the minutes before they were sent
  • A simple quality step on documents written as notes

Layouts

How the interface is arranged: pick one per tenant.

Project 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: the global navigation sits at the left and the sidebar of the area next to it. Every tenant picks its layout.

Typical uses

  • Tenants with many apps and many pages per app
  • Teams used to a tool with a persistent side navigation
  • Wide screens where the left rail costs nothing

Suite 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: apps open from a launcher grid and the pages of the area sit in a bar under the band, like an office suite.

Typical uses

  • Organisations that think in apps, not in menus
  • People who switch often between a few big apps
  • A clean screen with the content up front

Tabs 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: apps and pages are tabs under the band and a menu holds the pages of an app, like a browser.

Typical uses

  • People who keep several things open and move between them
  • Tenants with a few apps used all day
  • A familiar look for users who live in the browser

Workspace 1.0.1

A layout of the interface: a top bar with a wide search and a drawer that opens and closes, to give the content the whole screen.

Typical uses

  • Search-first work: finding things matters more than browsing
  • Small screens and laptops
  • Content-heavy pages such as documents and maps
