Plugins

The plugins of the signed catalog of the project. To install them from the Plugins page of your installation, add the catalog to its sources and trust its key:

mosaikit.marketplace.sources=https://mosaikit.github.io/catalog/
curl -fsSLo config/trusted-keys/mosaikit.pub.pem https://mosaikit.github.io/catalog/mosaikit.pub.pem
